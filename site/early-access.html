endr ← what mendr does

Early access · 50 builders · free · OpenAI shutdown: October 23

Get your code through October 23 with help.

On October 23, OpenAI shuts down 29 model ids, including gpt-4, gpt-3.5-turbo and o3-mini. Calls to them stop working that day, and some replacements reject parameters the old models accepted. mendr finds those calls and prepares the fix. Early access is me helping you through it.

  1. I run mendr on your repo with youWe find the calls to retiring models, including the ones in config, and I prepare the pull request, checked against your own build and tests. You approve it; nothing merges without you.
  2. Your own cases, on the old model and the new oneWrite a few real requests and what a good answer looks like. mendr sends them to both models with your key, and stops the change if the new one does worse.
  3. After you deploy, we check the feature togetherErrors, latency and cost before and after, from your own telemetry. And a direct line to the person building mendr: your questions decide what gets built next.
The tool itself is open source and free, and stays that way. Anyone can run it today: 
npx github:ajitheee/mendr#v0.5.11-alpha audit .

What's limited is the help, because it's one person. Spots go in sign-up order, starting now. Public launch: October 22.
